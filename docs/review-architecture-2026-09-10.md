## Verdict

Not ready to build from. Three blockers stop the data layer on day one: the reports and decisions the document delegates to are not on disk (B1), the 23rd array key `hitLocations` is simultaneously "an Item" and "an actor field" so the anatomy is emptied on every prepare (B2), and the item-before-actor preparation order makes every melee/encumbrance/dodge figure depend on data that does not exist yet when it is read (B3). Building the table-play layer as written produces wrong numbers in the two places players roll most: to-hit and skill bonuses applied twice (M3) and Malf 14 on undamaged weapons (M4), from a function that is not even in the bundle (M2). The manifest sets pointy-top hexes while claiming flat-top (M1), the droid workshop's per-index writes turn slot arrays into objects (M5), the U02 acceptance invariant cannot pass (M6), and the work plan cannot converge because the bootstrap, config, i18n, manifest and package files have no owner after wave 1 and check-all never sees new checks (M7, M8). Two of the four fixed-target fixtures exist only in someone's Downloads folder (M9). The export inventory, catalog counts, sweep/shock/wounding/FP/dodge semantics and the Rokarr target are correct and can be kept verbatim; the rest needs the fixes above before any unit starts.
