# Changelog

## 2.0.0 — 2026-09-10 (the v13 makeover)

A complete rebuild of the 2025 scaffold, which predated every ruling of the current rulebook
(`_husk/README.md` records what it was). Nothing of its rules code survives; the ShadowBase
website's engine is the rules implementation, bundled into the system.

### Added
- **Engine bundle** (`engine/shadowbase-engine.mjs`, built by `tools/build-engine.mjs` from `tools/engine-entry.ts`): the website's `getCalculatedStats`, the load path (`applyLoadMigrations`), every per-item derivation (`calculateBlasterStats`, `calculateMeleeWeaponStats`, `calculateLightsaberStats`, `calculateModifiedArmor`, `starshipDerivation`), roll targets and `resolveRollOutcome`, the 25-channel modifier bag, every catalog and every migration — 236 exports, proven at parity with the website's own load path (`check:engine-parity`).
- **Data layer**: the `character` Actor schema GENERATED from the website's zod schema (`tools/gen-actor-schema.mjs`; 99 fields, 24 nullable numbers, the 13 dead-echo keys skipped by name); 21 Item types each holding one website row verbatim (`system.row`); the adapter (`actorToSheet` / `sheetToActorData`) with a round-trip invariant over 73 corpus entries; hit locations kept on the actor; the two-pass derivation so melee/saber stats can read the owner's ST; status effects as ActiveEffects with empty `changes` and a row-less stun mirror; import/export through the website's own load path (Sahrhie 245 CP / HP 12 / Dodge 10 / BS 6; Hshif 241 / 13 / 9 / 6; the 2025 Kaelen file loads at the engine's 103, not its stored 178).
- **Table play**: 3d6 target rolls through `resolveRollOutcome` (368-cell grid pinned), attacks composed once (`attackSkillFor(...).skillTarget` + Min-ST shortfall as the target; global to-hit + gear bonus once as the modifier; `item.derived.malfunction` as the Malf), volleys, damage with the website formula translated to Foundry dice (min 1), unarmed strikes, defenses gated by facing/unready/stun, Force powers with a separate Apply Costs, techniques, crew actions, a stun-recovery roll (a declared departure), custom rolls; chat cards with Skill / Target / Rolled / Result / margin / malfunction / ghost-glitch lines and roll-mode support; the combat tracker with Basic Speed initiative (DX in the hundredths, never a die) and the round-driven turn counter + sweep on the active GM's client; the Tactical Damage Processor (typed DR, wounding multipliers, cold/stun/ion to EP, Shock effects, crippling flags, HT prompts).
- **Applications** (all ApplicationV2 + Handlebars): the actor sheet (header, five tabs with the website's 36 sections in order, blank → null form pipeline, whole-array writes for sparse rows, drag-drop of compendium kits / template Actors / JSON files); the Tactical HUD (Actions / Status / Handbook, roll history, the bell with pinned notifications, the settings gear); the handbook browser with the website's tiered search; 14 item-sheet families for the 21 types (slot builders with Direct Library Access GM-only, mods, ammunition, melee entries, the saber coupler, armor DR entries, ship systems, trait ladders, implant installs, Ch14 flaws); six sub-apps (Droid Workshop, Anatomy Workshop, Body Loadout, Suits & Sets, Crafting, Dossier Importer).
- **Compendia**: 32 packs in 10 folders — 2,220 Items (traits, skills, per-name powers/techniques/forms with `system.levels`, equipment, blueprints, armor and its parts/mods, weapon KITS that deal the weapon plus its parts on drop, ammunition, explosives, lightsabers, cybernetics, starships, vehicles, droid parts), 65 template Actors run through the load path, and the handbook as 24 JournalEntries / 169 pages with `@UUID` chapter links; names byte-identical to the catalogs; deterministic ids.
- **Settings** (`module/settings.mjs`): world `enforceEconomy` (reserved), `npcDefaultHidden`, `tokenRotationSync` (facing hexside ⇄ token rotation, idempotent both ways); client sheet preferences mirrored from the website (`compactRows`, `reduceMotion`, `showHandbookChips`, `showPointCosts`, `stickySectionHeaders`, `rememberOpenSections`, `keepRollHistory`, `rollHistoryDepth`, `notificationDepth`), applied as `data-*` attributes on every `.shadowbase` root; an "Open Tactical HUD" entry in the actor directory's context menu.
- **Styles**: the website's design tokens (`styles/variables.css`, 28 tokens), Foundry chrome restyled within `.shadowbase`, the component recipes (`sb-*`), sheet / tabs / HUD / items / chat / apps stylesheets; Font Awesome 6 Free through a 130-entry lucide → FA map.
- **Localization**: ~2,000 keys in `lang/en.json`; `check:i18n` holds templates ⇄ keys both ways.
- **Verification** (`node scripts/check-all.mjs`, 19 checks, all mutation-tested): engine-parity, actor-schema, adapter-round-trip, derived-not-stored, effects, esm, manifest, packs, handbook, rolls, templates, i18n, css-tokens, sheet-tabs, json-contract, hud, item-sheets, apps, settings — over a headless Foundry v13 shim. Preview renderers write every sheet, the HUD, every item sheet, the sub-apps and the settings panel to standalone HTML.
- **Docs**: `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` (the contract), `docs/WORKPLAN.md` (units + build log), `docs/REQUESTS.md` (cross-unit ledger), `docs/MANUAL-TEST.md` (the in-Foundry script), `docs/STYLE-SPEC.md`, the phase-1 digests, this changelog.

### Changed
- `system.json`: v13 manifest — `documentTypes` for the 22 sub-types, nine stylesheets, 32 packs + `packFolders`, `grid.type 4` (flat-top hex, 1 yd), token bars `resources.hp` / `resources.ep`, hot reload for css/hbs/json; no `htmlFields` (notes are plain textareas, as on the website).

### Removed
- The 2025 system (`template.json`, `scripts/*.js`, the old templates, stylesheet, `database/` and LevelDB packs, `TEST CHARACTERS/`) — moved to `_husk/`; `TEST CHARACTERS/kaelen rarr_shadowbase.json` became `fixtures/legacy-2025-kaelen-rarr.json`.

### Known limits (deliberate, documented)
- No Foundry is installed on the build machine: rendering, form binding, drag-drop, chat cards, the token HUD and sockets are verified by `docs/MANUAL-TEST.md`, not by the checks. Every Foundry v13 behaviour assumed without a client is marked UNVERIFIED in the code.
- Four website exports the sheets restate locally until the entry exports them (`equipmentPanes`, `attributeCharging` / `appliedModifier`, the blaster ammunition alias tables); the handbook loader shim's index shape; the website observations listed in `docs/REQUESTS.md` under "Fulllion".

### Book sync — 2026-10-03 (website `631ebe9`)
- **Reeling** (Chapter 7): below one third of maximum HP the sheet, the HUD and every Dodge roll show Move and Dodge halved, and the active effects list "Reeling (below 1/3 HP)" ("Damaged systems" for a droid). An active lightsaber Form's Dodge bonus joins the figure before it is halved, as on the website.
- **Damage past 1/2D** (Chapter 11): the Damage roll of a weapon that prints a paired range opens the roll prompt, which carries a "Past 1/2D" switch; Training-grade gas halves the roll by itself; the chat card and the roll history show each halving, and a volley halves every hit. An explosive round never halves.
- **Launchers**: the Merr-Sonn MM-s1 Mortar and the Czerka Underslung Grenade fire their loaded round like the other launchers (one test, the website's own).
- **Range** follows a fitted Focusing Emitter or Magnetic Accelerator Coils.
- **Species package revision 3**: a saved Miraluka gains the species' five lore lines once, with a "Species notes added" notice, on import and through the one-time world update, whose card now counts the actors it left untouched apart from the ones it marked.
- **Handbook and compendia** rebuilt from the book as of 2026-10-03: the range table's "1 yard or less" and "70 yards" rows, Infravision, Night Vision, Force Sight, a lit lightsaber's light, Meditation, the calendar's holidays.
- Still to be seen in a running Foundry (the checks cannot open one): the new switch in the roll prompt, the Halved lines on chat cards, the Reeling entry following a token's HP bar, and the world update on a world made from the earlier packs.
